# Hill Station Travel: SERP and fact research

Research carried out on 26 September 2026 with live web searches. Google's People Also Ask boxes and autocomplete could not be queried directly from this environment. The intent notes below come from:

- the result sets returned for each query;
- the page types that rank;
- the questions those pages answer.

Seasonal and access facts (permits, passes, e-passes, advisories) change often. They are taken from official portals where possible and framed on the pages as "check before you go".

## Deliberately left out or hedged

- **Prices** (packages, hotel rates, permit fees beyond "a fee applies") and **hotel names**, because they change and could look like endorsements.
- **Guaranteed snowfall.** Every snow statement is phrased as "most likely" with a month range, and readers are told to check forecasts.
- **Exact drive times.** Distances are approximate, and times are given as ranges with a caveat about traffic, weather and landslides.
- **Medical claims about altitude.** The pages give general acclimatisation practice and advise readers to consult a doctor; there is no dosing or medication advice.
