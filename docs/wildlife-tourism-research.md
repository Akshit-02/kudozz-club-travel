# Wildlife Tourism: research notes and sources

Research carried out on 25 and 26 September 2026 with live web searches. These notes record the time-sensitive facts the articles rely on, where they came from and how they are phrased. Seasons, closures, counts and booking rules can change. The articles give them with their date or say "check before you go", and they quote no prices.

## What ranks (SERP patterns)

- "wildlife tourism in India", "wildlife travel India" and "jungle safari India" return the same kinds of page: listicles of national parks, operator hubs and state tourism pages. We treat them as one intent, which the `/wildlife-tourism` hub owns.
- Park-name queries ("Ranthambore safari", "Kanha national park", "Kaziranga safari booking") are led by pages that combine the park guide, the zones and booking. Kudozz already has guides for these parks, so each guide keeps its intent.
- "best national parks", "best tiger reserves", "best wildlife sanctuaries" and "best wildlife destinations" each return a different listicle set (parks, tiger reserves, sanctuaries, and mixed species and destination lists). Each has its own pillar.
- Species queries (leopard safari, where to see rhinos, snow leopard tour) are served by operator guides that compare several places. That justifies a species page each. Lion queries are all about Gir, which the Gir guide and the Gujarat page cover.
- "wildlife in <state>" queries are strong for Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Kerala, Assam, Gujarat, West Bengal, Odisha and Tamil Nadu. Results for Goa and Andhra Pradesh are thin, so their content was folded into the sanctuaries and tiger-reserves pillars.

## Protected-area designations checked

- **National parks**, not sanctuaries: Keoladeo (Bharatpur), Sultanpur, Jaldapara (since 2014), Velavadar (Blackbuck National Park), Eravikulam, Keibul Lamjao and Mollem.
- **Sanctuaries:** Pobitora, Wayanad, Kumbhalgarh, Mount Abu, Bhimashankar, Chinnar, Thattekad (Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary), the Indian Wild Ass Sanctuary, and Bhagwan Mahavir and Cotigao in Goa.
- **Tiger reserves:** these are areas notified for tiger conservation, with a core and a buffer, and often include a national park and one or more sanctuaries. Examples are Kanha, Periyar and Satpura.
- **Neither:** Jawai is described as farmland and granite hills, not as a national park.

## Deliberately left out

- **Prices and fees.** They change often and differ between core and buffer zones and between Indian and foreign visitors. The articles tell readers to check the official portal.
- **Operator names and unofficial booking sites.** Many sites imitate the official portals, and the articles warn about this.
- **Any promise of sightings.** Every article states that sightings are never guaranteed.
- **Nagarjunsagar-Srisailam as its own page.** The tourism information we could verify was limited, so it is mentioned in best tiger reserves and held for manual review.
