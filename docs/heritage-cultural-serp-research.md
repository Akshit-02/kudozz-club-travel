# Heritage & Cultural Tourism: SERP and fact research

Research carried out on 26 September 2026 with live web searches. Google's People Also Ask boxes and autocomplete could not be queried directly from this environment. The intent notes below come from:

- the result sets returned for each query;
- the page types that rank;
- the questions those pages answer.

Where a fact is sensitive (UNESCO status, dates, closures, interpretations), we used the official source where one exists. Debated readings are labelled as such on the pages.

## Deliberately left out or hedged

- **Prices** (monument tickets, tours, tent city tariffs), because they change and are set by the ASI, states and operators. The pages point to official sites instead.
- **"Oldest", "largest" or "first" claims** unless a reliable source supports them. Where such a claim is common but debated, it is attributed:
  - "generally described as India's oldest museum" (the Indian Museum);
  - "traditionally regarded as one of the oldest mosques in India" (Cheraman Juma Masjid);
  - "according to long tradition" (Varanasi).
- **Year-specific festival dates**, except where announced officially (the Hornbill Festival and the 2026–27 Rann Utsav season). Other festivals are described by month or lunar calendar.
- **Firm dates for prehistoric rock art** (Bhimbetka) and exact counts that vary by source, such as the "seven cities" of Delhi. Both are presented as debated or variable.
