# Road Trips: search and fact research

Research carried out on 27–28 September 2026 with live web searches. Google's People Also Ask boxes and autocomplete could not be queried directly from this environment. The intent notes below come from three things:

- the result sets returned for each query;
- the page types that rank;
- the questions those pages answer.

Changeable facts are framed on the pages as approximate or "check before you go".

## Deliberately left out or hedged

- **Exact fees:** Ladakh's environment fee, Himachal's entry toll and Manali's green tax are described but not quoted.
- **Exact driving times:** given only as ranges.
- **Named hotels, operators, vehicles or drivers.**
- **Legal advice beyond widely known rules:** seat belts, helmets, no drink-driving, documents, FASTag.
- **Road-condition guarantees of any kind.**
