### [Amber Fort (Amer Fort), Jaipur: History and Visiting Guide](/blog/amber-fort)

**Quick answer (70 words):** Amber (Amer) Fort is about 11 km from central Jaipur and is part of the UNESCO Hill Forts of Rajasthan.
