# Nature Travel: SERP and fact research

Research carried out on 26 September 2026 with live web searches. Google's People Also Ask boxes and autocomplete could not be queried directly from this environment. The intent notes below come from:

- the result sets returned for each query;
- the page types that rank;
- the questions those pages answer.

Seasonal and status facts are framed on the pages as "check before you go".

## Deliberately left out or hedged

- **Superlatives such as "tallest", "longest" or "largest"** unless widely documented. Examples: Chilika is described as India's largest coastal lagoon; Majuli is described as "often described as" the largest river island; no "tallest waterfall" claims.
- **Exact rainfall or visitor figures.**
- **Named hotels or operators, and any eco-certification claims.**
- **Guaranteed blooms, water flow or sightings.**
