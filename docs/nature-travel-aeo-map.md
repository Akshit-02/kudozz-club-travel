### [The Western Ghats: A Nature Traveller's Guide](/blog/western-ghats-nature-travel)

**Quick answer (73 words):** The Western Ghats are a mountain range along India's west coast, inscribed by UNESCO in 2012 for their biodiversity. The best places to experience their nature are the Kaas plateau and Sahyadri forts in Maharashtra, Mollem and Dudhsagar in Goa, Agumbe, Kudremukh and Coorg in Karnataka, Silent Valley, Wayanad and Munnar's Eravikulam in Kerala, and the Nilgiris and Anamalais in Tamil Nadu. The monsoon brings waterfalls; October to March is best for walks.
