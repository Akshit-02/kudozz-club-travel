# Adventure Travel: research notes and sources

Research carried out on 25 September 2026 with live web searches. These notes record which time-sensitive facts the articles rely on, where they came from, and how they are phrased. Wherever a fact could change (seasons, closures, prices, operators, permits), the articles say "check before you go" or give it as "about", and we quote no prices.

## What ranks (SERP patterns)

- "adventure travel in India" and "adventure tourism in India" return the same kind of page: listicles of destinations and activities, operator hubs and the Ministry of Tourism's adventure portal. We treat them as one intent, owned by `/adventure-travel`.
- "best places for X in India" and "X in India" return the same listicles for rafting, paragliding, diving, skiing and camping, so each pair shares one URL.
- Place-activity queries ("paragliding in Bir Billing", "skiing in Gulmarg") are led by pages that are really destination guides centred on the activity. Kudozz already has such guides for Bir Billing, Gulmarg and Auli, so they keep that intent.
- Weekend queries ("adventure places near Delhi/Mumbai/Bangalore") show steady demand for Delhi, Mumbai (with Pune results overlapping) and Bengaluru. Hyderabad results are thin and mostly generic lists, so that page is held for manual review.

## Deliberately left out

- **Prices.** Search results give many conflicting prices, often from resellers. None are quoted.
- **Operator names**, except where a site is known by its operator (Jumpin Heights at Mohan Chatti and Mayem Lake, Della Adventure at Lonavala) and the operator's own published details were used.
- **Zipline lengths and heights** at Mawkdok, bungee heights at city crane jumps, and exact dive-site depths, which vary by source.
- **Stok Kangri status**, which has changed more than once; articles say to check.
