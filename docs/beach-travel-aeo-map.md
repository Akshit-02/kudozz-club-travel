### [Beach Safety in India: Flags, Rip Currents, Monsoon Rules and Marine Life](/blog/beach-safety-in-india)

**Quick answer (77 words):** To stay safe at Indian beaches, swim only at beaches with lifeguards, inside the flagged zone, and never when a red flag is up. Do not swim during the monsoon (June to September on the west coast) or in rough weather. If caught in a rip current, do not fight it: float, then swim parallel to the shore. Avoid rocks and cliff edges, do not swim after alcohol, and wear a life jacket for all water sports.
