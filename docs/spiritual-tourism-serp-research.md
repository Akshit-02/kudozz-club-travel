# Spiritual Tourism: SERP and fact research

Research carried out on 26 September 2026 with live web searches. Google's People Also Ask boxes and autocomplete could not be queried directly from this environment. The intent notes below come from the result sets returned for each query, the page types that rank and the questions those pages answer. Where a fact is sensitive (lists, counts, dates, rules) we used the official source where one exists, and we label traditions and disputed claims as such.

## Deliberately left out or hedged

- **Prices** (packages, helicopter fares, puja fees) and **darshan timings**. They change and many unofficial sites misstate them, so the pages point to the official temple or board sites.
- **Crowd figures and "richest temple" claims.**
- **One Jyotirlinga or Shakti Peetha list presented as the only correct one.**
- **Year-specific festival dates beyond those announced officially.** Rath Yatra, Janmashtami and Navratri are described by their lunar month, and the pages ask readers to check the year's date.
