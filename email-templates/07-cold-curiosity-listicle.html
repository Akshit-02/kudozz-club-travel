Quick list — 7 places in India that almost never come up when people plan a trip, but probably should:
1. Ajodhya Hills, West Bengal
A forested Purulia plateau with waterfalls and the tribal Chhau dance tradition — barely on the tourist map. Guide →
2. Munsiyari, Uttarakhand
A remote Kumaon town with an unobstructed view of the five Panchachuli peaks. Guide →
3. Sundarbans, West Bengal
The world's largest mangrove forest, and the only place you can (try to) spot a tiger by boat. Guide →
4. Gorumara National Park, West Bengal
A compact Dooars park with one of the highest one-horned rhino densities in India. Guide →
5. Tarapith, West Bengal
A Shakti Peeth pilgrimage town built around Bengal's Tantric tradition — intense, and genuinely unlike anywhere else. Guide →
6. Mukutmanipur, West Bengal
One of India's largest earthen dams, with a reservoir most people have never heard of, let alone visited. Guide →
7. Adi Kailash, Uttarakhand
"Little Kailash" near the Tibet border — a genuine, permit-gated multi-day journey for the very few who make it. Guide →
